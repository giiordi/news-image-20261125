"The World Today" — an entry for make.fun's "Sum up 25 November's world news in one image" bounty, $100 in USDC. A stdlib-only Python program renders a 1024x1024 front-page newspaper poster of Wednesday 25 November 2026: Pakistan's first astronaut reaches orbit aboard China's Shenzhou-24, the Houthis threaten Jeddah and the Saudi Red Sea ports, Kyiv says it hit a Russian cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland, chip stocks tumble as the AI rebound stumbles, Shanghai's party chief Chen Jining comes under investigation, and Bangladesh raises the garment minimum wage 36% after weeks of protests. All six stories were verified against Reuters, AP and WSJ reporting first published 24–25 November 2026.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program copies the committed `entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(default `output/`), and renders the same image from code if `entry.png`
is ever missing. Both runs write byte-identical output. No third-party
libraries, no network.

## The day in the image

1. **Space — China (lead):** Shenzhou-24 launched Wednesday morning with Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, 40, a Pakistan Air Force pilot — the first Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign astronaut aboard China's Tiangong station.

5. **China — Politics:** Shanghai Party chief Chen Jining under investigation — the fourth Politburo member probed since 2025; Shanghai's leadership pledged loyalty to Beijing.
6. **Bangladesh — Workers:** Garment minimum wage raised 36% to $139/month after weeks of protests that shut factories; takes effect 1 December.
